# Seam: world (governed egress)

This seam is not open. Nothing crosses it. To open it, answer the five
questions in doctrine/seams.md from evidence.

Standing regardless: every externally visible effect — send, publish,
pay, sign, delete of principal data — is class C and needs an approval
record in `workspace/80_governance/approvals/` naming the exact
effect. That is constitutional (entrance law 1) and opening this seam
cannot loosen it. An unlisted destination is denied, not evaluated.
